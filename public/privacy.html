When you submit an enquiry, All That Jazz Events & Entertainment Private Limited receives your name, email, company (if supplied), event type, timing and brief. We use these details to respond to your request and discuss your event.

Enquiries are stored on our server and are accessible to authorised studio administrators. The configured retention period is __RETENTION_DAYS__ days; older enquiries are removed by our scheduled maintenance process. Copies retained in backups follow the backup retention schedule.

The public experience saves your visited projects and arcade scores in your browser’s local storage. You can clear them through your browser settings. This website does not include advertising trackers. The private studio uses an essential session cookie for administrators.

Server access logs may contain IP addresses and request details for reliability and abuse prevention. We do not record your brief in application logs. External links, including Instagram, are governed by their providers’ privacy practices.

To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry, contact our team through @allthatjazz_atj ↗ and quote your enquiry reference. Please do not post private details publicly.
